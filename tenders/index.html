About this app

Updated weekly. Every entry links to the original notice, which alone is binding.

Google Calendar often refreshes subscribed feeds only after 12 to 24 hours. Your saved entries and visit data stay only on this device – no tracking, no account.

Calendar entries carry reminders 7 days and 1 day before the deadline. Deadlines are shown in the official time zone of the procedure. Offline, the app shows the data from your last visit.
