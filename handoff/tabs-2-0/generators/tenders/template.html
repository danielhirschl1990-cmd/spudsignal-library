About this app

Updated weekly. Every entry links to the original notice, which alone is binding.

Google Calendar often refreshes subscribed feeds only after 12 to 24 hours. Your saved entries and visit data stay only on this device – no tracking, no account.

Calendar entries carry reminders 7 days and 1 day before the deadline. Deadlines are shown in the official time zone of the procedure. Offline, the app shows the data from your last visit.

Coverage

Covered: TED (all EU/EEA above-threshold notices), BOAMP (France), simap.ch (Switzerland), ANAC (Italy), UK Find a Tender, EU Funding & Tenders Portal, and selected national funding programme pages.

Not covered yet, so this list is not complete:

  • Germany: national portal oeffentlichevergabe.de not covered – TED notices only
  • Austria: national portals not covered – TED notices only
  • Netherlands: TenderNed not covered – TED notices only
  • Poland: national portal not covered – TED notices only
  • Serbia, Türkiye: not covered

Every tender is re-checked for change notices (TED) and rectificatifs (BOAMP) on each update; entries not re-checked within 7 days are removed.
